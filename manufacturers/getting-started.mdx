Shapelid connects customers who need custom parts with verified manufacturers across Turkey. As a Partner, you get a steady stream of qualified manufacturing requests matched to your exact equipment and materials — no cold outreach, no bidding wars. This guide walks you through everything you need to do to get your account approved and your first job on the board.

## Who Can Apply

Shapelid accepts applications from manufacturers operating in Turkey who own and operate at least one of the following:

- **3D printing equipment** — FDM, SLA, SLS, MJF, or metal additive systems
- **CNC machining equipment** — 3-axis, 4-axis, or 5-axis milling, turning, or turn-mill centres

You do not need to be a large enterprise. Independent workshops and small-batch production shops are welcome, provided you can meet quality and delivery standards consistently.

## Onboarding Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit Your Application">
    Visit [partner.shapelid.com](https://partner.shapelid.com) and complete the manufacturer application form. You will be asked for your company name, tax ID, facility location, primary technologies, and a brief description of your production capacity. The form takes roughly ten minutes to fill out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Profile Review">
    The Shapelid Partner Operations team reviews every application manually. They verify that your stated capabilities match the technologies you have listed and that your facility is located within Turkey. Reviews are typically completed within 2–5 business days. You may be contacted by email if additional information is needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approval and Portal Access">
    Once approved, you will receive an email with your login credentials for the Partner Portal. Your account is initially set to a limited state until you complete your company profile — jobs will not appear in your Job Board until this step is done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Your Company Profile">
    Log in to the Partner Portal and navigate to **Settings → Company Profile**. Fill in every section: capabilities, certifications, equipment list, and production gallery. The more detail you provide, the more accurately Shapelid's matching engine can route the right RFQs to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Receiving Jobs">
    With your profile complete, your account becomes active. New RFQs that match your capabilities will appear on the Job Board in real time. You will also receive email and in-portal notifications whenever a new matching job is posted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Building a Strong Company Profile

Your company profile is the primary signal Shapelid uses to decide which jobs to show you. Take the time to fill it out thoroughly.

### Capabilities

List every manufacturing process you support and the tolerances you can reliably achieve. For 3D printing, include layer resolution ranges and supported part sizes. For CNC, include maximum workpiece dimensions, minimum feature sizes, and any multi-axis capabilities.

### Certifications

Upload copies of any quality or industry certifications your facility holds — ISO 9001, AS9100, IATF 16949, or similar. Certified manufacturers are prioritised for orders where customers have specified a certification requirement.

### Equipment List

Add each machine individually under **Settings → Machines**. For every machine, record the manufacturer, model, build volume or work envelope, and the materials it supports. Keeping this list accurate is critical — it feeds directly into job matching and capacity calculations.

### Production Gallery

Upload photos of finished parts, your production floor, and your quality control setup. Customers can see your gallery before a job is confirmed, and a well-presented gallery builds confidence in your work.

## The Verification Process

After you submit your application, Shapelid's team evaluates three main areas:

| Area | What Is Checked |
|---|---|
| **Equipment** | Machine models match stated capabilities; equipment is owned or under long-term lease |
| **Quality Processes** | Evidence of dimensional inspection tools, surface finish measurement, or documented QC procedures |
| **Capacity** | You have realistic production bandwidth for at least 2–4 concurrent jobs |

Shapelid may request photos of your facility or a short video walkthrough as part of this review. Providing this proactively in your application speeds up approval.

<Tip>
  Manufacturers who complete 100% of their company profile — including uploading at least five production gallery images and registering all active machines — receive up to three times more job matches than those with incomplete profiles. Spend the extra time upfront; it pays off quickly.
</Tip>
