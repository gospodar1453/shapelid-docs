## Response Time by Issue Type

Different types of requests are handled by different teams, so response times vary depending on what you need:

| Issue Type | Expected Response Time |
|---|---|
| Order status question | Within 4 business hours |
| Payment error or billing dispute | Within 4 business hours |
| Quality control complaint or reprint request | Within 1 business day |
| Manual quote review | Within 1 business day |
| File analysis or technical question | Within 1 business day |
| Partner application status | Within 2 business days |
| Enterprise / custom contract inquiry | Within 2 business days |
| General feedback or feature request | Within 3 business days |

Business hours are Monday through Friday, 09:00–18:00 Istanbul time (UTC+3). Inquiries received outside business hours are answered the following business day.

## What to Include in Your Support Request

The faster you give us the right context, the faster we can resolve your issue. Include the following in every support message:

<Steps>

  <Step title="Your order ID">
    Find this in the **Orders** section of [app.shapelid.com](https://app.shapelid.com). It looks like `ORD-XXXXXXXX`. Without this, we cannot look up your order in our system.
  </Step>

  <Step title="A description of the problem">
    Describe what you expected to happen and what actually happened. Include the exact error message if one was shown — copy and paste it rather than paraphrasing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="A screenshot">
    A screenshot of the error or unexpected state helps us diagnose the problem without going back and forth. Attach it directly to your email or drag it into the chat window.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Your CAD file (for upload or pricing issues)">
    If your issue relates to a file failing to upload, returning an unexpected price, or triggering a manual quote unexpectedly, attach the file to your email. We treat all uploaded files as confidential.
  </Step>

</Steps>

## Enterprise & Partnership Inquiries

If you represent a business with recurring or high-volume manufacturing needs, Shapelid can offer dedicated support including:

- A named account manager for your organisation
- Custom pricing agreements for predictable volume
- Priority queue access for time-sensitive production runs
- Custom reporting and invoice formats for procurement teams

Email [destek@shapelid.com](mailto:destek@shapelid.com) with **"Enterprise Inquiry"** in the subject line. Tell us about your industry, the technologies and materials you use most, and your approximate monthly order volume. We'll schedule a call to discuss options.

For manufacturing facilities interested in joining the partner network, visit [partner.shapelid.com](https://partner.shapelid.com) for a full overview of the onboarding process, partner benefits, and quality requirements.
