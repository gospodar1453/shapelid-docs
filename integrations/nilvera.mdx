---
title: "Nilvera"
description: "E-fatura (electronic invoicing) integration"
---

Nilvera handles marketplace e-fatura compliance for Turkish regulations.

## Features

- **Buyer invoice** — Auto-generated e-fatura sent to customer
- **Seller invoice** — Auto-generated e-fatura for manufacturer payment
- **Pazaryeri model** — Marketplace-compliant invoicing (MoR)
- **Auto-send** — Invoices sent automatically on order completion
