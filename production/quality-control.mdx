Before any Shapelid order is approved for shipping, it must pass a structured quality control process completed by the manufacturer. QC is not a formality — it's a documented, evidence-backed inspection tied directly to your payment release. You have full visibility into the results, and you retain the right to approve or dispute the outcome before funds are ever released.

## How the QC Process Works

When production and post-processing are complete, the manufacturer transitions your order to the **Quality Control** stage. At this point, they carry out a structured inspection specific to your part type (3D print or CNC machined) and submit an inspection report — including recorded measurements and photos — that becomes visible to you in your Client Portal.

No funds leave escrow and no shipping label is created until QC is logged and you approve the results.

## What Gets Checked

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Dimensional Accuracy" icon="ruler">
    Critical dimensions specified in your design file are measured and recorded. The manufacturer logs actual measured values against your tolerances so you can verify conformance at a glance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Surface Finish" icon="sparkles">
    Surface quality is visually and tactilely assessed against the finish specification you selected (e.g., as-printed, sanded, polished, anodised). Any deviations are noted.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Material Verification" icon="flask">
    The material used is confirmed against your order specification. For CNC jobs this includes material grade; for 3D prints this includes filament or resin type and colour.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Visual Inspection" icon="eye">
    The part is checked for warping, cracks, layer delamination, tool marks, burrs, incomplete features, and any other visible defects. Inspection photos are taken from multiple angles.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Viewing Your QC Report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Order Detail Page">
    Go to [app.shapelid.com](https://app.shapelid.com), navigate to **My Orders**, and click your order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Quality Control Tab">
    Once your order reaches the QC stage, a **Quality Control** tab appears on the order detail page. Click it to open the full inspection report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Checklist and Measurements">
    The report shows each checklist item with a pass/fail status. For dimensional checks, you'll see a table with the specified value, the measured value, and the tolerance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Inspection Photos">
    Scroll down to the **Inspection Photos** section to see images of your finished part taken during QC. You can click any photo to enlarge it or download the full set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Approving Your Order

After reviewing the QC report, you'll see an **Approve & Release Payment** button at the top of the Quality Control tab. Clicking this confirms that you're satisfied with the inspection results. Once you approve:

1. The funds held in escrow are released to the manufacturer.
2. A shipping label is generated via Geliver cargo.
3. Your order moves to the **Shipped** stage and a tracking number becomes available.

<Warning>
  You have **72 hours** from the time the QC report is submitted to approve or raise a dispute. If no action is taken within 72 hours, the order is automatically approved and payment is released. Make sure your notification emails are active so you don't miss the QC approval window. If you need more time to review, contact [destek@shapelid.com](mailto:destek@shapelid.com) before the window closes.
</Warning>

## Raising a Quality Issue

If the inspection report reveals a problem, or if the photos show that the part does not meet your specifications, you can open a dispute instead of approving.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click 'Raise a Quality Issue'">
    On the Quality Control tab, click the **Raise a Quality Issue** button instead of approving. This pauses the approval timer immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the Issue">
    Fill in the dispute form. Be as specific as possible — reference the checklist item or dimension that is out of spec, and attach any supporting files or annotated photos if you have them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shapelid Reviews the Dispute">
    Our quality team reviews your dispute within one business day. We may request additional photos from the manufacturer or ask clarifying questions from both parties.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolution">
    Depending on the outcome, the resolution may be a remake at no extra cost, a partial or full refund, or a mutual agreement to accept the part with a discount. Escrow funds remain held until the dispute is resolved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For complex disputes or urgent matters, reach out directly to [destek@shapelid.com](mailto:destek@shapelid.com) with your order number and a summary of the issue.
