Once you approve your QC report, Shapelid automatically generates a shipping label and your manufacturer prepares your package for collection. From that moment, your shipment is trackable in real time directly from the Client Portal — no need to visit a third-party courier website.

## How Shipping Works

Shapelid ships all domestic orders via **Geliver**, a Turkish cargo network with broad coverage across all 81 provinces. When your order is handed to Geliver:

- A tracking number is assigned and synced to your order automatically.
- Your order status updates to **Shipped**.
- You receive a shipping notification email containing your tracking number and a direct tracking link.

Tracking status is kept up to date inside the Client Portal, so the status you see there is always current.

## Tracking Your Shipment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Client Portal">
    Sign in at [app.shapelid.com](https://app.shapelid.com) and go to **My Orders**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Your Order">
    Click the order you want to track. Once it has shipped, a **Shipping** tab will appear on the order detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Live Tracking">
    Click the **Shipping** tab to see the full shipment timeline — including pickup confirmation, transit hub scans, and estimated delivery date — all synced directly from Geliver.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the Tracking Number Directly">
    Your Geliver tracking number is shown at the top of the Shipping tab. You can copy it and use it on the Geliver website or app if you prefer to track there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Typical Delivery Times

Delivery times are estimates based on the destination province and depend on Geliver's standard service levels. Shapelid does not control courier timelines once a package is in transit.

| Destination | Estimated Delivery |
|---|---|
| Istanbul (same-city) | 1 business day |
| Major cities (Ankara, Izmir, Bursa, Antalya) | 1–2 business days |
| Other provinces | 2–3 business days |
| Remote or rural addresses | 3–4 business days |

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding Turkish public holidays. Orders shipped on a Friday may not be collected until the following Monday.

## Packaging Standards

Manufacturers on Shapelid are required to package parts according to guidelines designed to protect precision and fragile components in transit:

- **Rigid parts** are wrapped in bubble wrap and packed in a double-walled corrugated box with void fill.
- **Flexible or thin-walled prints** are individually wrapped and separated with foam inserts to prevent contact.
- **Multi-part orders** have each piece individually wrapped and labelled before being packed together.
- **Metal CNC parts** are wrapped to prevent surface scratches and, where applicable, packaged with desiccant to prevent moisture damage.

If your order has specific packaging requirements (e.g., anti-static bags for electronic enclosures), note them in the special instructions field when placing your order.

## If Your Package Arrives Damaged

If the outer packaging or the parts themselves show damage on arrival, act quickly:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Photograph Everything Before Unpacking Further">
    Take clear photos of the box exterior, the packing materials, and the damaged parts before moving anything. These photos are essential for any damage claim.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Report Within 48 Hours">
    Log in to the Client Portal, open the order, and click **Report a Delivery Issue** on the Shipping tab. Upload your photos and describe the damage. Reports made more than 48 hours after the recorded delivery time may not be eligible for a claim.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shapelid Contacts Geliver">
    Our team will open a cargo damage claim with Geliver on your behalf and keep you updated. Depending on the outcome, we will arrange a replacement production run or issue a refund.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For urgent delivery issues, email [destek@shapelid.com](mailto:destek@shapelid.com) with your order number and photos attached.

<Note>
  Shapelid currently supports domestic shipping within Turkey only. International shipping is not available at this time. If you are based outside of Turkey, you will need to provide a Turkish delivery address — such as a freight forwarder or a local contact — to receive your order. We plan to add international shipping options in a future update.
</Note>
