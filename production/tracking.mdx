Once you place an order on Shapelid, you can follow its entire journey through production in real time. Every status change — from the moment a manufacturer accepts your job to the day your package arrives — is reflected in your Client Portal and communicated via email, so you're never left wondering where your parts are.

## Production Stages

Your order moves through eight distinct stages. Each transition is logged with a timestamp and, where applicable, photos uploaded by your manufacturer.

| Stage | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Order Placed** | Your order has been submitted and payment is held in escrow. Shapelid is matching your job to verified manufacturers. |
| **Manufacturer Assigned** | A verified Turkish manufacturer has accepted your job. You can see their profile in the order detail page. |
| **Material Ordered** | The manufacturer has sourced and confirmed the required material for your part. |
| **In Production** | Active machining or 3D printing is underway. Expect progress photos at key milestones. |
| **Post-Processing** | Finishing work is being applied — sanding, painting, anodising, thread tapping, or other specified treatments. |
| **Quality Control** | The manufacturer is completing the digital QC checklist, recording measurements, and uploading inspection photos. |
| **Shipped** | Your package has been handed to Geliver cargo. A real-time tracking number is now visible in your order. |
| **Delivered** | Geliver has confirmed delivery to your address. |

## Where to Find Your Order Status

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Client Portal">
    Navigate to [app.shapelid.com](https://app.shapelid.com) and sign in to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to My Orders">
    Click **My Orders** in the left sidebar. All active and past orders are listed here with their current stage displayed as a badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Order Detail Page">
    Click on any order to open its detail view. You'll see a visual progress timeline at the top of the page showing your order's current position across all stages, with timestamps for each completed step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View the Activity Feed">
    Below the timeline, the **Activity Feed** shows a chronological log of every status change, manufacturer note, and uploaded photo.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Progress Photos

Manufacturers upload production photos at key milestones during your order. These photos appear in the **Photos** tab on your order detail page and in the Activity Feed. You can expect photos at the following points:

- **Material confirmation** — showing the raw material before work begins
- **Mid-production** — showing your part during machining or printing
- **Post-processing** — showing the part after finishing treatments
- **QC inspection** — showing dimensional checks and the finished part

Photos are uploaded directly by the manufacturer and are timestamped. You can download any photo from the Photos tab for your own records.

## Email Notifications

Shapelid sends you an email every time your order moves to a new stage. Notification emails include:

- The new stage name and a short description of what it means
- Any notes left by the manufacturer
- A direct link back to your order in the Client Portal

You can adjust which email notifications you receive in **Account Settings → Notifications**. We recommend keeping production stage alerts enabled so you don't miss QC approval windows.

<Note>
  If your order appears stuck at a stage for longer than expected — for example, if it remains in **Material Ordered** for more than two business days without an update — contact Shapelid support at [destek@shapelid.com](mailto:destek@shapelid.com) with your order number. Our team will follow up with the manufacturer and provide you with an update within one business day.
</Note>
