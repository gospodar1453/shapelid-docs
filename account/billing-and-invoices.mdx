Shapelid processes all payments securely through PayTR, one of Turkey's most widely used payment gateways. Your money is never paid directly to a manufacturer at checkout — instead, it's held in escrow until your order passes quality control and you approve the result. This model ensures you're protected throughout the entire production process.

## Payment Methods

Shapelid accepts the following payment methods at checkout:

- **Credit and debit cards**: Visa, Mastercard, and Troy cards are all supported through PayTR's secure payment infrastructure. Card details are tokenised and never stored on Shapelid's servers.
- **Taksit (instalment payments)**: Turkish credit cards from major banks are eligible for interest-free or low-interest instalments at checkout. Available instalment options (e.g., 3, 6, or 9 months) are displayed dynamically based on your card's bank and the order total. Instalment availability is determined by your card issuer, not by Shapelid.
- **Bank transfer**: Available for large or enterprise orders. Contact [destek@shapelid.com](mailto:destek@shapelid.com) to arrange a transfer-based order.

All transactions are processed in Turkish Lira (TRY).

## How the Escrow Model Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="You Pay at Checkout">
    When you confirm your order, the full order amount is charged to your payment method via PayTR. The funds are held securely in escrow — they are not transferred to the manufacturer at this point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Production Proceeds">
    The manufacturer is notified of the confirmed order and begins work. They can see that payment is secured, but cannot access the funds until the order is approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Quality Control Is Completed">
    After production, the manufacturer completes the QC checklist and uploads inspection photos. You receive a notification to review the results in the Client Portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You Approve the Order">
    Once you've reviewed the QC report and are satisfied with the results, you click **Approve & Release Payment**. The escrowed funds are released to the manufacturer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dispute Protection">
    If you're not satisfied, you can raise a quality issue instead of approving. Funds remain held in escrow throughout the dispute process and are only released — to you or the manufacturer — once the dispute is resolved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## E-Invoices (e-Fatura)

Shapelid issues electronic invoices (e-fatura) for every order through **Nilvera**, a certified Turkish e-invoice service provider. Your invoice is automatically generated when your order is approved and payment is released.

To view and download your invoice:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Order Detail Page">
    Go to **My Orders** in the Client Portal and click the relevant order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Documents Tab">
    Select the **Documents** tab on the order detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download Your Invoice">
    Click **Download Invoice** to save the e-fatura as a PDF. The invoice is also sent to the email address registered to your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you need your invoice reissued with updated company details (e.g., a different tax number or trade name), contact [destek@shapelid.com](mailto:destek@shapelid.com). Note that e-fatura corrections must comply with Turkish Revenue Administration (GİB) rules and may require a cancellation and reissuance.

## Viewing Your Billing History

All past payments and invoices are available in one place:

1. Sign in to the Client Portal at [app.shapelid.com](https://app.shapelid.com).
2. Click **Account** in the sidebar, then select **Billing & Invoices**.
3. Here you'll see a full list of all transactions, including the order reference, date, amount, payment method, and invoice download link for each.

You can filter the list by date range or export it as a CSV for accounting purposes using the **Export** button.

<Note>
  All Shapelid invoices are issued in compliance with Turkish tax regulations. Invoices include the applicable VAT (KDV) rate — currently 20% for manufacturing services — and are issued under Shapelid's registered tax number. If your company is VAT-registered and needs to reclaim input VAT, your accountant can use the e-fatura PDF or the GİB e-fatura portal to verify the document. For questions about VAT treatment on your specific orders, consult your tax adviser or contact us at [destek@shapelid.com](mailto:destek@shapelid.com).
</Note>
