The Client Portal gives you a central place to manage every order you've placed on Shapelid — whether it's tracking an active production run, reordering a part you've had made before, downloading your invoice, or cancelling an order that hasn't entered production yet. Everything you need is available without contacting support.

## Accessing the Orders Section

Sign in at [app.shapelid.com](https://app.shapelid.com) and click **My Orders** in the left-hand sidebar. This page lists all your orders — both active and completed — in reverse chronological order. Use the search bar to find an order by its reference number, or filter by status using the dropdown at the top of the list.

Click any order to open its detail page, where you'll find the full production timeline, photos, QC report, shipping information, and available actions.

## Order Statuses Explained

| Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Order Placed** | Your order has been placed and payment is held in escrow. Manufacturer matching is in progress. |
| **Manufacturer Assigned** | A verified manufacturer has accepted the job. Production preparation is underway. |
| **Material Ordered** | The manufacturer has confirmed and sourced the required material. |
| **In Production** | Your part is actively being manufactured. |
| **Post-Processing** | Finishing treatments (e.g., painting, sanding, anodising) are being applied. |
| **Quality Control** | The manufacturer has submitted an inspection report for your review. Your approval is required before shipping can proceed. |
| **Shipped** | Your package is in transit with Geliver cargo. A tracking number is available. |
| **Delivered** | Geliver has confirmed delivery to your address. |
| **Disputed** | A quality issue has been raised. Funds are held pending resolution. |
| **Completed** | The order is fully closed. Payment has been released. |
| **Cancelled** | The order was cancelled before production began. A refund has been issued. |

## Reordering a Previous Part

If you need more of a part you've already had made, you can reorder directly from the order detail page without uploading your files again.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Completed Order">
    Go to **My Orders** and click the completed order for the part you want to reorder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Reorder">
    Click the **Reorder** button at the top right of the order detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Same Spec or Modify">
    - **Same Spec**: reorders the exact part with the same material, finish, quantity, and tolerances. The quote is generated instantly.
    - **Modify Spec**: opens the order configuration screen with your previous settings pre-filled, allowing you to change quantity, material, finish, or upload a revised file before getting a new quote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and Confirm">
    Review the quote and confirm payment. Your new order is created and enters the matching queue immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you order the same part regularly, save it as a **Favourite** by clicking the star icon on the order detail page. Favourited parts appear at the top of your My Orders list and in a dedicated Favourites section, making reorders even faster. You can also set up a repeat order schedule for high-frequency parts — contact [destek@shapelid.com](mailto:destek@shapelid.com) to discuss standing order arrangements.
</Tip>

## Cancelling an Order

You can cancel an order only while it is in **Order Placed** or **Manufacturer Assigned** status — before the manufacturer has ordered material or started production. Once the order moves to **Material Ordered** or beyond, cancellation is no longer available.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Order">
    Navigate to **My Orders** and click the order you wish to cancel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Cancel Order">
    Scroll to the bottom of the order detail page and click **Cancel Order**. This button is only visible if the order is still eligible for cancellation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Cancellation">
    A confirmation dialog will appear. Confirm the cancellation. The escrowed payment is released back to your original payment method within 3–5 business days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your order has already entered production and you need to discuss cancellation due to exceptional circumstances, contact [destek@shapelid.com](mailto:destek@shapelid.com) as soon as possible. Late-stage cancellations may incur a partial charge to cover material and labour costs already incurred.

## Downloading Invoices and Production Files

All documents related to your order are stored on the order detail page and are available to download at any time, even after the order is completed.

- **E-Invoice (e-fatura)**: Click the **Documents** tab on your order detail page, then click **Download Invoice**. Invoices are issued through Nilvera and are fully compliant with Turkish e-fatura regulations.
- **Design Files**: Your uploaded design files (STL, STEP, DXF, etc.) are stored under the **Files** tab. Click **Download** next to any file to retrieve it.
- **QC Report**: Available as a PDF download from the **Quality Control** tab once the inspection is complete.
- **Shipping Label & Tracking Confirmation**: Available under the **Shipping** tab after your order ships.

To download all documents for an order at once, click the **Download All Documents** button on the Documents tab.
