Your Shapelid account settings give you control over everything tied to your account — from the delivery address and company details used on your invoices, to which email notifications you receive, to your saved payment methods and security preferences. All settings are managed from a single location in the Client Portal.

## Accessing Account Settings

Sign in at [app.shapelid.com](https://app.shapelid.com) and click **Account** in the left-hand sidebar. From the Account menu, select **Settings**. The settings page is organised into sections — Profile, Notifications, Payment Methods, and Security — which you can navigate using the tabs at the top.

## Profile Information

Your profile details are used to pre-fill delivery addresses, populate your invoices, and identify your account with Shapelid support.

To update your profile:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Profile Tab">
    Open **Account → Settings → Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit Your Details">
    Update any of the following fields:
    - **Full name** — used on invoices and account communications
    - **Company name** — included on e-fatura if you're ordering as a business
    - **Tax number (Vergi No)** — required for B2B e-fatura issuance
    - **Phone number** — used for delivery coordination if needed
    - **Default delivery address** — this address is pre-filled at checkout but can be overridden per order
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Changes">
    Click **Save Changes**. Your updated information takes effect immediately for new orders. Already-issued invoices are not retroactively updated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Keeping your company name and tax number accurate is important for e-fatura compliance. If you need to correct details on a previously issued invoice, contact [destek@shapelid.com](mailto:destek@shapelid.com).

## Notification Preferences

Shapelid sends email notifications to keep you informed about your orders and account. You can control which notifications you receive from the **Notifications** tab in Account Settings.

| Notification Type | Default | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| Order stage updates | On | Keep on |
| QC report ready (approval required) | On | Keep on |
| Shipping and delivery updates | On | Keep on |
| Dispute status updates | On | Keep on |
| Promotions and platform news | On | Your preference |
| New manufacturer assignment | On | Your preference |

Toggle any notification type on or off using the switches on the Notifications tab, then click **Save Preferences**.

<Note>
  We strongly recommend keeping **QC report ready** notifications enabled. You have a 72-hour window to approve or dispute your QC report before payment is automatically released. Missing this notification may result in automatic approval of an order you haven't reviewed.
</Note>

## Saved Payment Methods

Shapelid uses PayTR's card tokenisation system to let you save payment methods securely for faster checkout. Your actual card number is never stored on Shapelid's servers — only a secure token is retained.

To manage your saved cards:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Payment Methods Tab">
    Open **Account → Settings → Payment Methods**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a New Card">
    Click **Add Card** and enter your card details. The card is tokenised immediately and stored securely via PayTR.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a Default Card">
    Click **Set as Default** next to any saved card to make it your default payment method at checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a Card">
    Click **Remove** next to any card you no longer want saved. This action cannot be undone, but you can re-add the card at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Password Change and Security

To change your password, go to **Account → Settings → Security**. Enter your current password, then enter and confirm your new password. Passwords must be at least 8 characters and include a mix of letters and numbers. Click **Update Password** to save.

If you've forgotten your current password, sign out and use the **Forgot Password** link on the login page to receive a reset email.

We also recommend:

- Using a unique password not shared with other services.
- Avoiding shared devices when accessing the Client Portal.
- Contacting [destek@shapelid.com](mailto:destek@shapelid.com) immediately if you believe your account has been accessed without your permission.

## Deleting Your Account

If you wish to permanently delete your Shapelid account, go to **Account → Settings → Security** and scroll to the **Delete Account** section. Click **Request Account Deletion** and confirm your request.

Please note the following before requesting deletion:

- Account deletion is **permanent and irreversible**. All your order history, saved files, and invoices will be removed from the Client Portal.
- You must have **no active or disputed orders** before your account can be deleted. Resolve all open orders first.
- E-fatura records are retained by Shapelid in accordance with Turkish tax law (minimum 10 years) regardless of account deletion. These records are not accessible via the portal after deletion but can be requested by contacting [destek@shapelid.com](mailto:destek@shapelid.com) if needed for legal or tax purposes.
- Your deletion request is processed within 30 days.
