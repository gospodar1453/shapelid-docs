## Simple Mode vs. Advanced Mode

To keep the ordering experience fast for most users, the material selector opens in **Simple Mode** by default. Simple Mode shows 17 curated materials — one or two high-value options per technology — chosen to cover the most common engineering and prototyping requirements:

| Technology | Simple Mode Material(s) |
|---|---|
| FDM | PLA, ABS |
| SLA | Standard Resin, Engineering Resin |
| SLS | PA12 (Nylon 12) |
| MJF | PA12 |

## Material Categories

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Plastics / Polymers">
    Thermoplastic and photopolymer options cover everything from quick-turn concept models to chemically resistant end-use parts.

    ### PLA — Polylactic Acid
    The most widely used FDM material. PLA is easy to print, dimensionally stable at room temperature, and available in dozens of colours. It is plant-derived and recyclable, making it a good choice for presentations, educational models, and low-stress prototypes. Not recommended for applications above 55 °C or in contact with solvents.

    ### ABS — Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene
    A workhorse thermoplastic with better impact resistance and higher heat deflection than PLA (~100 °C HDT). ABS is suitable for functional enclosures, automotive clips, and parts that will be acetone-vapour smoothed for a high-gloss surface finish.

    ### PETG — Polyethylene Terephthalate Glycol
    Combines the ease of printing of PLA with improved chemical resistance and slight flexibility. PETG is food-contact safe when printed correctly and works well for fluid-handling components, clear covers, and snap-fit assemblies.

    ### PC — Polycarbonate
    High-temperature thermoplastic (HDT ~130 °C) with excellent impact strength and optical clarity in unfilled grades. Used for jigs, fixtures, transparent housings, and parts that need to survive elevated temperatures in service.

    ### TPU Soft — Thermoplastic Polyurethane
    Flexible, rubber-like material available in Shore hardness from 85A to 95A. TPU is ideal for gaskets, grips, wearable devices, vibration-dampening mounts, and over-mould simulations.

    ### PA12 (SLS / MJF) — Nylon 12
    The standard material for SLS and MJF. PA12 offers excellent fatigue resistance, low moisture absorption compared to PA6, good chemical resistance, and a wide operating temperature range (−40 °C to 100 °C continuous). Available plain or glass-bead filled for higher stiffness.

    ### Photopolymer Resins (SLA / Polyjet)
    Shapelid's SLA catalogue includes Standard White/Grey Resin, Transparent Resin, Engineering Resin (ABS-like), High-Temp Resin (HDT 289 °C), Castable Resin for lost-wax casting, and Dental-grade biocompatible resins. Polyjet adds rubber-like materials from Shore 27A to 95A and multi-material composite options.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Metals">
    Shapelid's metal catalogue spans aluminium alloys, stainless steels, tool steels, copper alloys, and titanium, available across CNC Turning, CNC Milling, EDM, Laser Cutting, Bending, and DMLS.

    ### Aluminium Alloys
    - **Aluminium 6061-T6** — The most popular CNC aluminium. Excellent machinability, good corrosion resistance, weldable. Used for structural brackets, housings, heat sinks, and aerospace non-critical components.
    - **Aluminium 7075-T6** — Higher strength than 6061 (tensile ~500 MPa), used where weight-to-strength ratio is critical (drones, bicycle parts, motorsport components). Harder to weld but machines beautifully.
    - **AlSi10Mg (DMLS)** — Powder-bed fusion aluminium alloy with good strength-to-weight ratio. Standard choice for DMLS aluminium components.
    - **Aluminium 5052-H32 (Sheet)** — Excellent formability and corrosion resistance; standard sheet metal choice for enclosures and marine applications.

    ### Stainless Steels
    - **SS304** — The most widely used stainless steel for sheet metal (Laser Cutting / Bending) and CNC milling. Food-safe, corrosion-resistant, and cost-effective.
    - **SS316L** — Enhanced corrosion resistance thanks to molybdenum addition. Required for marine, chemical processing, and medical device applications. Available for CNC and DMLS.
    - **SS303** — Free-machining stainless for high-volume turned parts (shafts, fittings). Not weldable.

    ### Tool Steels
    - **H13 Hot-Work Tool Steel** — Excellent thermal fatigue resistance and toughness at elevated temperatures. Standard for die-casting tooling, moulds, and EDM cavities.
    - **D2 Cold-Work Tool Steel** — High hardness (up to 62 HRC after heat treatment) with good wear resistance. Used for punches, dies, and cutting tools.

    ### Titanium
    - **Ti6Al4V (Grade 5)** — The aerospace and medical titanium alloy. High strength-to-weight ratio, excellent corrosion resistance, biocompatible. Available for DMLS and CNC Milling. Priced on the live titanium spot rate.

    ### Brass and Copper
    - **Brass C360** — Free-machining brass for turned fittings, connectors, and decorative parts.
    - **Copper C101** — High conductivity copper for electrical and thermal components; machinable on CNC Turning/Milling.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Engineering & Advanced">
    Advanced Mode unlocks specialist materials for high-performance applications where standard plastics and common alloys are not sufficient.

    ### DMLS High-Performance Alloys
    Beyond SS316L and AlSi10Mg, Shapelid's DMLS partners process:
    - **Inconel 718** — Nickel superalloy for extreme heat and corrosion environments (jet engine components, heat exchangers). Retains strength up to 700 °C.
    - **17-4 PH Stainless Steel** — Precipitation-hardening stainless with tensile strength up to 1310 MPa after H900 heat treatment. Used in aerospace fittings and high-load brackets.
    - **Inconel 625** — Superior corrosion resistance in seawater and acidic environments; used in offshore and chemical plant components.
    - **Maraging Steel 1.2709** — Ultra-high-strength tool steel for conformal cooling inserts and injection mould tooling.

    ### Polyjet Multi-Material
    Polyjet allows you to combine rigid and rubber-like resins in a single build, with Shore hardness gradients achievable in a single print job. Typical applications include:
    - Over-mould prototypes that simulate a rubber grip on a rigid housing
    - Medical device ergonomic studies
    - Soft robotics and wearable device prototypes

    ### Glass- and Carbon-Fibre Filled Polymers
    Available for FDM (Markforged-compatible) and SLS:
    - **PA12-GF (Glass-Filled)** — 30–40% glass bead fill increases stiffness and reduces warping.
    - **CF-PLA / CF-Nylon** — Short-fibre carbon-filled FDM materials for increased stiffness in printed parts.

    ### PEEK and High-Performance Polymers
    PEEK (Polyether Ether Ketone) is available for FDM on high-temperature machines maintained by select Shapelid partners. With a continuous service temperature of 250 °C and excellent chemical resistance, PEEK is used in semiconductor equipment, aerospace cabin components, and medical sterilisable parts.

## Live Market-Based Pricing

Material prices on Shapelid are not fixed catalogue rates — they are computed dynamically every day from four real-time data sources:

| Data Source | Commodities Covered |
|---|---|
| **metals.dev API** | Aluminium (Al), Copper (Cu), Nickel (Ni), Zinc (Zn) spot prices in USD/kg |
| **Yahoo Finance — HRC=F** | Hot-Rolled Coil steel futures; applies to mild steel, SS304/316 sheet |
| **Yahoo Finance — BZ=F** | Brent crude oil; used as proxy for polymer/plastic resin feedstock costs |
| **TCMB (Central Bank of Turkey)** | USD/TRY exchange rate for Turkish Lira conversion |

Each morning, Shapelid's pricing engine fetches the latest values from these sources, computes a per-material cost rate in TRY/cm³ (with currency conversion applied), and updates the instant quoting calculator. Because markets can move between the time you view a quote and the time you check out, the final price is locked at the moment of order confirmation.

<Note>
  Prices shown at checkout reflect the real-time market rate at the moment you confirm your order. Quotes generated earlier in the same session may differ slightly if commodity prices moved during your browsing session. The locked order price will always be displayed clearly before you enter payment.
</Note>

## Material Options and Finishes

Depending on the technology and material you choose, additional configuration options may be available in the order form:

| Option | Available For | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Surface Finish** | FDM, SLA, CNC | Options include as-built, sanded, primed, painted, anodised (Al), powder-coated (steel) |
| **Colour** | FDM, SLA, MJF, Polyjet | Colour options vary by material; RAL colours available for powder coating |
| **Layer Resolution** | FDM, SLA | Finer layers improve surface quality but increase machine time and cost |
| **Tolerance Class** | CNC Turning/Milling | ISO 286 fit classes (H7/h6 etc.) or custom tolerances accepted on drawing |
| **Certification** | All technologies | ISO 9001, AS9100D, ISO 13485 (medical), RoHS, material test reports — triggers manual quote |
| **Heat Treatment** | CNC, DMLS | Annealing, hardening, stress relieving — quoted as a line item |
| **Thread Type** | CNC Turning/Milling | Metric (M), UNC/UNF, BSP — specify in your STEP file or attached drawing |
