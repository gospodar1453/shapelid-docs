Selecting the right manufacturing process is the single most important decision you'll make before placing an order. The technology you choose determines achievable tolerances, surface finish, material options, lead time, and cost. Shapelid offers 11 verified production technologies across three categories — 3D Printing, CNC Machining, and Sheet Metal — all fulfilled by vetted Turkish manufacturers.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="3D Printing">
    Shapelid supports six additive manufacturing technologies, covering everything from inexpensive prototypes to flight-qualified metal components.

    ### FDM — Fused Deposition Modelling

    FDM extrudes thermoplastic filament layer by layer, making it the most accessible and cost-effective 3D printing process. It is ideal for concept models, jigs, fixtures, and functional prototypes where fine surface finish is not critical.

    | Attribute | Detail |
    |---|---|
    | **Best For** | Rapid prototyping, large structural parts, low-cost iteration |
    | **Typical Materials** | PLA, ABS, PETG, PC, TPU, ASA, Carbon-Fibre filled PLA |
    | **Accepted File Format** | STL, OBJ, GLTF |
    | **Layer Resolution** | 0.1 – 0.4 mm |
    | **Build Volume** | Up to 300 × 300 × 400 mm (varies by partner machine) |

    ---

    ### SLA — Stereolithography

    SLA cures liquid photopolymer resin with a UV laser, producing parts with smooth surfaces and very fine feature detail. It is the go-to process for display models, dental applications, and intricate mechanical prototypes.

    | Attribute | Detail |
    |---|---|
    | **Best For** | High-detail prototypes, clear parts, dental and medical models |
    | **Typical Materials** | Standard Resin, Engineering Resin, Castable Resin, Dental Resin |
    | **Accepted File Format** | STL, OBJ, GLTF |
    | **Layer Resolution** | 0.025 – 0.1 mm |
    | **Note** | Parts require post-cure; overhangs need supports |

    ---

    ### SLS — Selective Laser Sintering

    SLS fuses nylon powder with a CO₂ laser without support structures, enabling complex geometries and nested builds. Parts have a slightly grainy surface but excellent mechanical properties and chemical resistance.

    | Attribute | Detail |
    |---|---|
    | **Best For** | Functional end-use parts, snap fits, living hinges, complex geometries |
    | **Typical Materials** | PA12 (Nylon 12), PA11, Glass-Filled PA12, TPU |
    | **Accepted File Format** | STL, OBJ, GLTF |
    | **Layer Resolution** | 0.1 mm |
    | **Min Wall Thickness** | 0.7 mm recommended |

    ---

    ### MJF — Multi Jet Fusion

    HP's MJF process jets fusing and detailing agents onto nylon powder and fuses them with an infrared lamp. It produces parts faster than SLS with more consistent mechanical properties throughout the part.

    | Attribute | Detail |
    |---|---|
    | **Best For** | Production-grade polymer parts, high-volume functional prototypes |
    | **Typical Materials** | PA12, PA12 Glass Beads, PA11, TPU |
    | **Accepted File Format** | STL, OBJ, GLTF |
    | **Layer Resolution** | 0.08 mm |
    | **Surface Finish** | Slightly smoother than SLS; grey by default, dyeable |

    ---

    ### DMLS — Direct Metal Laser Sintering

    DMLS fuses metal powder with a high-powered fibre laser, layer by layer, inside an inert atmosphere. The result is fully dense metal parts with mechanical properties comparable to wrought stock. It is Shapelid's highest-capability additive process.

    | Attribute | Detail |
    |---|---|
    | **Best For** | Metal functional parts, aerospace brackets, tooling inserts, medical implants |
    | **Typical Materials** | SS316L, AlSi10Mg, Ti6Al4V, Inconel 718, 17-4 PH Stainless |
    | **Accepted File Format** | STL, OBJ, GLTF |
    | **Layer Resolution** | 0.02 – 0.05 mm |
    | **Note** | Support removal and heat treatment included in Shapelid's price |

    ---

    ### Polyjet

    Polyjet inkjets multiple photopolymer resins simultaneously and cures them with UV light, enabling multi-material and multi-colour parts in a single build. Shore hardness can be mixed continuously in the same part.

    | Attribute | Detail |
    |---|---|
    | **Best For** | Over-mould simulation, multi-colour display models, ergonomic grips |
    | **Typical Materials** | Rigid Resin, Rubber-like (Shore 27A–95A), Transparent Resin, Simulated ABS |
    | **Accepted File Format** | STL, OBJ, GLTF |
    | **Layer Resolution** | 0.016 mm (16 µm) |
    | **Note** | Support material is soluble; complex internal channels are cleanable |

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="CNC Machining">
    CNC machining removes material from solid stock using computer-controlled cutting tools. Shapelid offers three subtractive processes, all programmed from your STEP or IGES file.

    ### CNC Turning

    The workpiece rotates against a stationary cutting tool on a lathe, making it the most efficient process for cylindrical, conical, and rotationally symmetric parts such as shafts, bushings, and threaded fittings.

    | Attribute | Detail |
    |---|---|
    | **Best For** | Shafts, bushings, sleeves, fittings, pins, threaded components |
    | **Typical Materials** | Aluminium 6061/7075, Brass, SS303/304/316, Mild Steel, Tool Steel |
    | **Accepted File Format** | STEP, IGES |
    | **Typical Tolerance** | ±0.01 mm on turned diameters |
    | **Max Diameter** | 300 mm (live tooling available on select partners) |

    ---

    ### CNC Milling

    The cutting tool rotates while the workpiece is held stationary (or indexed), making it suitable for prismatic parts, pockets, slots, and complex 3D contours. Shapelid's partners support 3-axis and 5-axis milling.

    | Attribute | Detail |
    |---|---|
    | **Best For** | Housings, enclosures, brackets, moulds, complex contoured surfaces |
    | **Typical Materials** | Aluminium 6061/7075, SS304/316, Tool Steel D2/H13, Titanium, Delrin, HDPE |
    | **Accepted File Format** | STEP, IGES |
    | **Typical Tolerance** | ±0.025 mm standard; ±0.01 mm with fine finishing |
    | **Max Part Size** | 600 × 500 × 300 mm (3-axis); consult for 5-axis |

    ---

    ### EDM — Electrical Discharge Machining

    EDM erodes material using controlled electrical sparks between the workpiece and an electrode. It is the process of choice for hardened steels, very narrow slots, and features that cannot be reached by a rotating cutter.

    | Attribute | Detail |
    |---|---|
    | **Best For** | Hardened tool steel, narrow keyways, complex die cavities, blind slots |
    | **Typical Materials** | Hardened Tool Steel, Carbide, Inconel, Titanium |
    | **Accepted File Format** | DXF (wire-path geometry), STEP for context |
    | **Typical Tolerance** | ±0.005 mm (wire EDM) |
    | **Note** | Parts must be electrically conductive; quoting typically requires manual review |

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Sheet Metal">
    Sheet metal operations start from flat DXF profiles and transform them into 2D cut parts or 3D formed components. Both processes use standard sheet stock available from Shapelid's manufacturer network.

    ### Laser Cutting

    A high-powered CO₂ or fibre laser cuts flat sheet profiles at high speed. Shapelid's partners process steel, aluminium, stainless steel, and brass sheet in gauges from 0.5 mm to 25 mm.

    | Attribute | Detail |
    |---|---|
    | **Best For** | Flat panels, brackets, gaskets, decorative grilles, chassis parts |
    | **Typical Materials** | Mild Steel, SS304/316, Aluminium 5052/6061, Brass, Copper |
    | **Accepted File Format** | DXF |
    | **Typical Tolerance** | ±0.1 mm on cut profiles |
    | **Max Sheet Size** | 3000 × 1500 mm |

    ---

    ### Bending

    After laser cutting, flat blanks are formed into 3D shapes using a CNC press brake. Shapelid quotes bending as a sequential operation on an already-cut DXF profile, with bend lines indicated as construction geometry.

    | Attribute | Detail |
    |---|---|
    | **Best For** | Enclosures, brackets, flanges, frames, structural channels |
    | **Typical Materials** | Mild Steel, SS304/316, Aluminium 5052/6061 |
    | **Accepted File Format** | DXF (flat pattern with bend lines) |
    | **Typical Tolerance** | ±0.5° on bend angles; ±0.2 mm on flange lengths |
    | **Note** | Include K-factor notes in your DXF or drawing for precision assemblies |

  </Tab>
</Tabs>
