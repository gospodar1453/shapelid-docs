## How It Works

Getting a part manufactured on Shapelid takes just a few steps. The platform handles pricing, manufacturer matching, production tracking, and payment — all from your browser.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload Your CAD File">
    Log in to the [Client Portal](https://app.shapelid.com) and upload your design file. Shapelid accepts STL, OBJ, GLTF, STEP, IGES, and DXF formats. The platform's Kernel engine analyzes your geometry automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Technology & Material">
    Select the manufacturing technology that fits your part — such as FDM for quick prototypes, SLS for complex geometries, or CNC Milling for precision metal parts. Then pick from 135+ materials with live pricing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get an Instant Quote">
    Shapelid calculates your price in real time based on material cost, machine time, setup fees, and overhead. No waiting for a sales rep — the quote appears immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place Your Order">
    Review the pricing breakdown, confirm your delivery address, and complete payment securely via PayTR. Your funds are held in escrow until production is confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track Production">
    Once your order is accepted by a verified manufacturer, you can monitor production status in real time from your dashboard. You'll receive updates at each stage of the process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Delivery">
    Your finished parts are shipped directly to you. Once delivered and confirmed, the escrow payment is released to the manufacturer.
  </Step>
</Steps>
