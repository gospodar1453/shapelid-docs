## Prerequisites

Before you begin, you'll need:

- A CAD file (STL, OBJ, GLTF, STEP, IGES, or DXF)
- A Shapelid account ([sign up here](https://app.shapelid.com))
- A modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload your file">
    Go to [app.shapelid.com](https://app.shapelid.com) and upload your CAD file. The system automatically detects the format and runs geometric analysis.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a technology">
    Select from 11 production technologies: FDM, SLA, SLS, MJF, DMLS, CNC Turning, CNC Milling, EDM, Laser Cutting, Bending, or Polyjet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select material and options">
    Pick from 135+ materials. Configure finish, color, resolution, tolerance, and certification options based on the selected technology.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your instant quote">
    The pricing engine calculates material cost, machine time, setup, and overhead. Adjust quantity to see volume pricing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place your order">
    Review the quote and place your order. Track production status, QC results, and delivery — all from the Client Portal.
  </Step>
</Steps>
