Shapelid is designed to get you from CAD file to confirmed quote as fast as possible. This guide walks you through every step — from creating your account to reviewing a full pricing breakdown — so you can place your first order with confidence. The entire process typically takes less than five minutes.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have the following ready:

- **A CAD or design file** in one of the supported formats:

  | Format | Supported For |
  |--------|--------------|
  | STL | 3D printing (all technologies) |
  | OBJ | 3D printing (all technologies) |
  | GLTF | 3D printing (all technologies) |
  | STEP | CNC machining (Milling, Turning, EDM) |
  | IGES | CNC machining (Milling, Turning, EDM) |
  | DXF | 2D processes (Laser Cutting, Bending) |

- **A Shapelid account** — [sign up free at app.shapelid.com](https://app.shapelid.com). No credit card is required to get a quote.

If your design is not yet exported from your CAD tool, export it in the appropriate format for your intended process before continuing.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up or Log In">
    Go to [app.shapelid.com](https://app.shapelid.com) and create a free account, or log in if you already have one. After logging in, you'll land on your **Orders Dashboard** where all of your active and past orders are listed.

    Click **New Order** or **Get a Quote** to begin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Your CAD File">
    Drag and drop your file onto the upload area, or click **Browse Files** to select it from your computer. Shapelid accepts STL, OBJ, GLTF, STEP, IGES, and DXF files.

    Once uploaded, the Kernel engine automatically analyzes your file's geometry — calculating volume, surface area, bounding box dimensions, and identifying any potential issues. You'll see a 3D preview of your part rendered in the browser.

    <Note>
      If your file contains errors (non-manifold geometry, missing faces, or zero-thickness walls), Shapelid will flag the issue and suggest corrective action before you proceed.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Manufacturing Technology">
    Choose from the 11 available technologies. Each option displays a short description and typical use cases to help you decide:

    - **FDM** — Low-cost thermoplastic prototypes and functional parts
    - **SLA** — High-detail resin with smooth surface finish
    - **SLS** — Complex geometries, no support structures
    - **MJF** — Consistent production-ready nylon parts
    - **DMLS** — High-strength metal, aerospace and medical grade
    - **Polyjet** — Multi-material, color, visual prototypes
    - **CNC Milling** — Precision machined metal and plastic
    - **CNC Turning** — Cylindrical parts from bar stock
    - **EDM** — Hardened metals, intricate internal features
    - **Laser Cutting** — 2D sheet metal and plastic
    - **Bending** — Sheet metal forming

    If you're unsure which technology is right for your part, visit the [Technologies guide](/ordering/technologies) for a detailed comparison.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a Material and Configure Options">
    After selecting a technology, you'll see the available materials for that process. Shapelid offers two selection modes:

    **Simple Mode** — Browse materials by category (e.g., Standard Plastics, Engineering Resins, Metals). Select a material and the platform fills in standard parameters automatically. This is the fastest path to a quote.

    **Advanced Mode** — Unlock full control over infill density, layer height, wall thickness, surface finish, post-processing options, and more. Use this mode when your part has specific mechanical or aesthetic requirements.

    All material prices reflect live market rates and are updated regularly. You can see the per-unit price adjust in real time as you change options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Your Pricing Breakdown">
    Before placing an order, Shapelid shows you a full, itemized pricing breakdown so there are no surprises:

    | Line Item | What It Covers |
    |-----------|---------------|
    | **Material Cost** | Raw material consumed by your part, calculated from geometry |
    | **Machine Cost** | Estimated machine time at the rate for the selected technology |
    | **Setup Fee** | One-time preparation cost per order (bed leveling, fixturing, etc.) |
    | **Overhead** | Platform and operational costs allocated per order |
    | **Total** | Sum of all line items, shown with VAT where applicable |

    You can adjust quantity, infill, finish, or material on this screen and watch the total update instantly. If you need multiple quantities, use the quantity selector to see per-unit pricing at volume.

    <Note>
      For certain complex parts — unusually large geometries, specialized alloys, or multi-step post-processing — the platform may not be able to generate an instant quote. In these cases, you'll see a **Request Manual Quote** button. Fill in your requirements and a Shapelid specialist will respond within one business day.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place Your Order">
    When you're satisfied with the quote, click **Place Order**. You'll be prompted to:

    1. Confirm or enter your delivery address
    2. Select a shipping option (standard or express, where available)
    3. Complete payment via **PayTR**

    Your payment is held in escrow — it is not released to the manufacturer until your order is accepted and production begins. Once the order is placed, you'll receive a confirmation email and your order will appear in your dashboard with a live status tracker.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens Next

After placing your order, a verified manufacturer is assigned to your job. You can track production status in real time from the **Orders** section of your dashboard. Status updates progress through the following stages: **Confirmed → In Production → Quality Check → Shipped → Delivered**.

When your parts are delivered and you confirm receipt, the escrow payment is released to the manufacturer automatically.
