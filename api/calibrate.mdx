The `/calibrate` endpoint is **not available for general public use**. It is an internal tool used by authorized Shapelid integration partners to help maintain pricing accuracy for specific production environments.

If you believe your organization qualifies for access, contact [destek@shapelid.com](mailto:destek@shapelid.com) with a description of your integration and use case. Unauthorized requests to this endpoint will be rejected.
