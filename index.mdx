Shapelid is an on-demand manufacturing platform that connects customers with verified manufacturers across 11 production technologies. Upload your CAD files, get instant pricing, and manage the entire production lifecycle — from quote to delivery.

<Tip title="What you can do with Shapelid">
- **Instant Pricing** — Upload STL, OBJ, STEP, or DXF files and get real-time quotes
- **11 Technologies** — FDM, SLA, SLS, MJF, DMLS, CNC Turning, CNC Milling, EDM, Laser Cutting, Bending, Polyjet
- **Material Database** — 135+ materials with live pricing from metals.dev, Yahoo Finance (HRC=F, BZ=F), and TCMB exchange rates
- **Manufacturer Network** — Verified Turkish manufacturers with capacity management and QC tracking
</Tip>
